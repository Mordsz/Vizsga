Frequently Asked Questions

What is "The Damn Dam" about?

The Damn Dam is a turn-based survival game, where you have to protect the dam that keeps the city above the “3 Malicious Islands”.
You play as a high ranking knight who been task'd with a single goal: destroy anyone or anything who pose a threat to the dam.

What is the main gameplay?

  • Gather resources
  • Defend the citizens and their peace from monsters
  • Upgrade or repair the city's Dam
  • Explore the separated islands

How can I download the game?

You can download the game by clicking the "Download" button in the navigation menu on any page.

What platforms is the game available on?

The game is currently available on Windows

What language is “The Damn Dam” coded in?

C#
